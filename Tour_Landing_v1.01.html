TOURS

A simple, integrated system for organising touring events.

TD Tour brings together the practical tools needed to prepare and run a Tour — entrant administration, route mapping, roadbook schedules, including Tulips and optional live tracking — in one package.

Routes can be planned on the mapping page or gathered using the Field Tool App. The mapped route then imported into the mapping page, saved, then loaded into Schedule. Distances are available in kilometres and miles.

The system is designed for touring events where clear route information and simple administration are the priority. During the event entrants can be tracked and monitored using mobile apps.
